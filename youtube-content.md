# YouTube Content Skill

## Purpose
Build complete YouTube content packages for Shorts and long videos.

## Core outputs
Depending on the request, create:
- video concept
- hook
- title options
- opening sequence
- script
- shot list
- B-roll plan
- voiceover
- on-screen text
- description
- chapters
- CTA
- pinned comment
- tags and hashtags
- thumbnail concept

## Title rules
- Make the promise clear.
- Avoid misleading claims.
- Put important search terms naturally near the front when appropriate.
- Do not add "4K" unless it is materially relevant and requested.

## Shorts
Target fast comprehension. The first seconds should establish the topic or curiosity gap quickly. Every beat should serve retention or clarity.

## Long-form
Use strong opening context, logical progression, pattern changes, and useful payoff. Avoid padding.

## Thumbnail
Use one clear subject, strong visual contrast, minimal text, and an obvious focal point. Do not rely on text alone to explain the video.
