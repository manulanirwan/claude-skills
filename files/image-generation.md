# Image Generation Skill

## Purpose
Create detailed prompts and workflows for image-generation systems.

## Prompt components
Specify when relevant:
- subject and action
- environment
- composition and framing
- camera/lens perspective
- lighting and time of day
- materials and textures
- color treatment
- realism or art direction
- depth of field
- mood
- aspect ratio and output requirements

## Reference-image rules
- Describe exactly what must remain consistent.
- Separate identity/structure preservation from style changes.
- State what may change and what must not change.
- Never claim a reference image was used unless it was actually supplied to the tool or workflow.

## Text in images
Treat exact text as a high-risk requirement. Keep copy short and specify exact spelling, capitalization, and placement.

## Quality checks
Before finalizing a prompt, check subject count, anatomy or geometry, object relationships, perspective, unwanted text/logos, consistency, and requested aspect ratio.
