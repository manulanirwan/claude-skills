# Writing Skill

## Purpose
Create clear, useful writing matched to the requested audience, purpose, platform, and tone.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, purpose, format, length, and tone.
2. Preserve user-provided facts and names exactly unless asked to change them.
3. Lead with the most important point.
4. Use short active sentences.
5. Remove repetition, filler, and vague claims.
6. Structure long content with meaningful headings.
7. Keep terminology consistent.
8. Check grammar, logic, factual consistency, and requested constraints.

## Content rules
- Never invent missing facts.
- Mark assumptions clearly.
- Do not pad to hit a word count; add useful substance instead.
- For persuasive writing, distinguish evidence from rhetoric.
- For technical writing, define unfamiliar terms before relying on them.

## Output modes
Support articles, scripts, reports, SOPs, documentation, proposals, emails, social posts, and other prose requested by the user.
