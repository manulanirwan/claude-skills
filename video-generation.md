# Video Generation Skill

## Purpose
Create production-ready prompts for AI video generation.

## Shot design
For each clip define when useful:
- duration
- aspect ratio
- subject and action
- environment
- camera position
- camera movement
- lens or focal look
- lighting
- motion direction and speed
- transition or edit point
- sound design
- dialogue or voiceover
- on-screen text

## Continuity
Across multiple clips, lock:
- character identity
- wardrobe
- vehicle/object identity
- environment when required
- time of day
- lighting logic
- visual style
- camera logic

## Retention
For short-form content, prioritize a visible hook early, rapid visual change where justified, clear escalation, and a strong ending. Do not add random cuts simply to increase speed.

## Safety and realism
Do not present physically impossible actions as realistic unless the user requests fiction. Flag dangerous stunts when relevant.
